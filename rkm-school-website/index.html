About Our School

Welcome to R.K.M.S.D. Education Centre

Located in Hardev Nagar, Bhitargaon Road, Saadh, Kanpur Nagar, Uttar Pradesh, our institution is committed to providing excellent education while preserving Indian cultural values.

We offer a unique educational structure:

  • Play Group to Class 5: English Medium
  • Class 6 to 12: Hindi Medium

Our school focuses on holistic development, ensuring that every student receives personalized attention in a disciplined environment.
